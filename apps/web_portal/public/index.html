chta / private chat

Chat with people you know.

Share messages, photos, videos, audio, and files with friends whose identities you have checked. Start in your browser and bring your identity with you using your recovery words.

Before you begin

Keep the 24 words you receive when you create an identity.

They are how you restore it on another device or after losing browser data. Keep them private and somewhere you can find them.

Inside the app

Your chats, contacts, and identity.

Web app preview at desktop and phone sizes. All accounts and messages shown are sample data.

Private by design

Your conversations stay with you.

Messages and files are end-to-end encrypted

Content is encrypted between the devices in a conversation. Relays forward encrypted data without being able to read your messages or files.

No central chat archive

Your history and cached media stay on your devices. The discovery seed does not store chat or file content.

No ads or app analytics

No advertising or analytics trackers are built into the app. You do not need an email address or phone number to create an identity.

The seed uses temporary connection metadata to find peers and limit abuse. Peers and network providers can still see IP addresses.

Getting started

From first visit to first chat

You and your friend each need an identity. Plan a moment to compare fingerprints through a channel you already trust.

  1. 01

    Create or restore your identity

    Open the app and choose Create new identity. If you already have one, choose Restore identity and enter its 24 word recovery phrase.

  2. 02

    Save your recovery words

    Write down all 24 words in order and complete the confirmation in the app. Anyone with those words can unlock your identity, so do not send them to a friend.

  3. 03

    Set up your profile

    Open Profile to add a display name and, if you like, a photo. Your friend will see these when you share your contact card.

  4. 04

    Add a friend

    One person scans or pastes the other's card in Contacts and selects Add friend. The other person chooses Review → Accept. Keep both apps open; requests retry from the sender's device. Compare fingerprints through a channel you trust when needed.

  5. 05

    Open the conversation

    Choose your friend in Chats and keep both apps open while they connect. Type a message or use the attachment button to choose media and files. Messages support Markdown formatting.

Once you're connected

How a chat connects

Open your friend's chat to send a message. The connection shown in the app tells you how it is reaching them.

Direct

Your devices connect to each other. Both people need to be online for a Direct connection. Some networks block it; keep both apps open and try again if it does not connect.

Relay

A reachable public relay can carry encrypted chat when Direct is unavailable. In Connect, scan a relay QR code or use a public relay shared in your contact card. A relay route still needs to be reachable.

Files

Files up to 25 MiB can use the relay. Larger files, up to 10 GiB, wait for Direct and can resume after an interruption. Browser storage limits and available disk space still apply.

The discovery seed helps friends find each other and exchange connection signals. It does not store your messages or files.

Keep access

Changing device or browser?

Your browser keeps local app data under the site address where you used it. Data from another address will not appear here automatically. Your 24 word recovery phrase restores your identity keys. Contacts and chat history stay on each device, so keep any files you need separately.
